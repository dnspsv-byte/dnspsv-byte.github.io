KickPong Goal — Privacy Policy

Português · English · Español

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains how the game KickPong Goal (package com.kickpong.kickpong_goal), published on Google Play, handles your data.

1. Data the game stores itself

The game has no sign-up, no login and no server of its own. The information below stays only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • the name you choose to show on the scoreboard (optional);
  • preferences: language, sound, AI difficulty and screen rotation for 2 players;
  • match statistics (wins and win streak against the AI);
  • the date the app was first opened (used for the "Remove Ads" welcome price).

To delete this data, uninstall the game or clear the app data in Android settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads between matches using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID and other identifiers;
  • approximate location, derived from the IP address;
  • interactions with ads and with the app;
  • performance information and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (maximum rating PG).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Where the law requires consent (such as the European Union), the game shows Google's consent form, which can be reopened in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy.

3. In-app purchases

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store any payment data; only the confirmation that the purchase exists, used to turn off ads and for "Restore purchases".

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data, other than the processing by Google for ads and purchases described above.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.
